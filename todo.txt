how the OTP direkt import works (summary):
- the user supplies as_session, str_sess and kw_caac (bank account id). No cookie and no browser headers needed.
- step 1: POST f_knjizenja with the full form body (as_session, str_sess, lang, lista_drop, kw_caac, datum_od, datum_do, sort, iznos_od, iznos_do, opis, action=m_stanje_racuna).
  The server stores the account + date range server-side and creates a new transaction_id (e.g. -110) on every search ("Prikazi" click).
  It is returned in the response inside the DataTables script: "transaction_id": "-110" (flags is "8;true").
- step 2: GET f_knjizenja_get_page with as_session, str_sess, transaction_id, flags, start, length. kw_caac and the dates are NOT sent here;
  the transaction_id alone decides which account and date range are returned. Page with start=0,100,200... until a page has fewer than length rows.
- the transaction_id is per search: never reuse it for another account/range and never persist it, scrape it and use it immediately.
